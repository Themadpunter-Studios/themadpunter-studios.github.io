NULLIARDS is a fast-paced physics roguelike where you blast billiard balls off a constantly changing table
while dodging increasingly dangerous threats.

Knock all the balls off of the map, then choose modifiers to complicate your run in the intermission. 
Some modifiers will complicate the game, some modifiers will spawn entities, and all are extremely challenging.
After you choose your modifier, it's back to the fun of billiards, dodging, and ultimately, survival.

Notable Features:
* Billiards-style physics - Launch a ball around a table and knock the other balls off
* Roguelike modifiers - Choose enemies, rule changes, game variations, and more after each round
* Dangerous enemies - Run from various monsters, dodge their attacks, and survive the round
* Fast-paced arcade gameplay - Built for speed, fast decision making, and precision
* Original soundtrack - Multiple original drum and bass tracks built for the game

About the Developer
Themadpunter, or Noah, is a teenage indie game developer based out of Wisconsin. He's been making
games of varying quality since he was 8, and his goal as a developer is to make cool games that you
can play over and over and never get tired of.
