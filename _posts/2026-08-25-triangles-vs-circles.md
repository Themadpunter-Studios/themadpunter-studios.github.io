A new webcomic of mine is in the works, Triangles VS Circles. You can read it [here](https://trianglesvscircles.themadpunter

Triangles VS Circles is a webcomic about a war between the Triangles and Circles, each who settled on opposite sides of
Eclipse Island. But they both want to control the whole island so they have been at war for a long time. But the Circles
make an underhanded move to end the war by killing the Triangles' president, which leads to a whole new level of war...
